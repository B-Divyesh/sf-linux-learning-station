Privacy

Short version: a child can use every core activity without an account. We do not collect names, drawings, answers, progress, identifiers, analytics, or advertising data.

Data on this device

The chosen age range and activity results are stored in this browser. An adult can export a progress file, import one, or erase everything from Adult tools.

Purchases

Buying the ₹499 bundle opens hosted checkout. The station does not send age range, answers, drawings, or progress to checkout.

The station stores a returned or restored license token and its latest validity result in this browser. License verification sends only that token to the Sociobot API at most once per day.

Offline and third parties

The installed station caches its own files for offline use. Core activities load only this site’s files, with no third-party fonts, scripts, ads, chat, or trackers. Buying opens hosted checkout. Verifying a license sends its token to the Sociobot API.

Your choices

Use Export data before moving devices. Use Erase progress to delete local learning data. For purchase questions, email support@sociobot.in. For privacy questions, email privacy@sociobot.in.
